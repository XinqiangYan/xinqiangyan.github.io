## Overview

Conventional receive arrays need cables, preamplifiers and vendor-specific connectors, which adds cost and complexity. The Wireless Litzcage uses inductive coupling to transfer signal to the scanner's built-in coil. This removes the cable and connector and makes the coil simpler, cheaper and easier to deploy.

Our stacked-loop coupling design increases power-transfer efficiency, and the Litzcage geometry provides inherent detuning during transmit.
