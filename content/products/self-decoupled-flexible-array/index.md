## Overview

In conventional arrays, neighboring elements must be overlapped or connected through decoupling networks. That limits where elements can go and makes flexible designs hard to build. Our **self-decoupled coil** balances the electric and magnetic coupling between elements so that they decouple by themselves. This allows lightweight, flexible arrays that wrap closely around the anatomy.
