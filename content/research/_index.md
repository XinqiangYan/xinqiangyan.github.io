## About the lab

Yan Lab develops innovative engineering solutions for technical challenges in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and MRI-guided therapies. Our key research areas include improving RF and B0 homogeneity, reducing RF heating near implants, reducing motion-related artifacts, accelerating acquisition, and raising the signal-to-noise ratio of MRI systems. We also develop portable MRI scanners and MRI-guided therapy systems.

Dr. Yan has led multiple NIH-funded projects and invented influential devices such as the **self-decoupled coil** and the **RF-transparent B0 shimming coil**, which have advanced both MRI technology and its clinical applications. The lab includes research staff scientists, postdocs, PhD students and undergraduate students.

## Current projects

1. Solving dark bands in tcMRgFUS using passive reflective antennas
2. Next-generation integrated RF Tx/Rx and B0 shimming coils for 7T brain and spinal cord MRI
3. Flexible RF coils and baluns
4. RF-transparent B0 shimming coil arrays
5. Low-cost wireless coils for clinical scanners
6. Novel materials and devices that reduce RF heating near MRI implants
