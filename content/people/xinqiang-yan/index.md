My research develops engineering solutions to the technical challenges of MRI. My main goals are improving RF and B0 homogeneity in high-field MRI, accelerating acquisition, reducing RF heating near implants, and increasing the signal-to-noise ratio.

I have led multiple NIH-funded projects and invented the **self-decoupled coil** and the **RF-transparent B0 shimming coil**.

### Current projects

1. Solving dark bands in tcMRgFUS using passive reflective antennas
2. Next-generation integrated RF Tx/Rx and B0 shimming coils for 7T brain and spinal cord MRI
3. Flexible RF coils and baluns
4. RF-transparent B0 shimming coil arrays
5. Low-cost wireless coils for clinical scanners
6. Novel materials and devices that reduce RF heating near MRI implants
